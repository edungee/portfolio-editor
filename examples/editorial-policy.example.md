## Meaningful changes

Include completed implementation, a confirmed decision that changes direction, an experiment with documented learning, or an independently verified public release. Ignore cosmetic edits, repeated discussion and plans without a decision. Describe a prototype as a prototype. Do not infer adoption, impact or launch from code alone.

## Evidence and dates

Use only the exact sources and scopes in my private configuration. Separate proposals from decisions and completed work. Use the actual event date. Defer claims with missing dates, conflicting evidence or unclear outcomes. Retain citations and source quotes only in the private review unless explicitly cleared below.

## Disclosure

Default to private. Clear public wording only for facts already present in approved public artifacts or facts I explicitly authorize for public use. Do not expose source IDs, private URLs, messages, customer names, personal details, credentials or confidential metrics. Public rationale is public content too. Defer uncertain claims and explain what clarification is needed. Public previews require publication-cleared content.

## Tone and review

Use first-person, plain language and concrete outcomes. Avoid inflated impact claims and unsupported numbers. Keep summaries short. Proposals remain private for my review; this policy does not authorize publishing, PR creation, merging or deployment.
